Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information Qline collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. Qline is an app operated by Jazz Sajonia in Davao City, Philippines ("Qline", "we", "us"). Contact: chrono07g@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. If you choose Continue with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID instead of a password (scopes openid, email, profile).
  • The organisations you create or join, your role and invitations.

Business content you add

  • Tasks, projects, comments, contacts, notes, documents, files you import (CSV or Excel), workflows, approvals and AI conversations.
  • An audit log of important actions in your organisation.

AI provider keys

  • If an owner or admin adds an API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI or DeepSeek, the key is stored encrypted and used only to make requests for your organisation.

Connected accounts (optional)

If you connect Google, Microsoft or Slack, we store encrypted access tokens and the account's email and name. What we access depends on the features you turn on. Google data is described in section 3.

Technical information

  • Basic request logs (such as time, IP address and error details) kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.
  • App notifications: Qline shows alerts on your phone (for example an important email or a new lead). The app checks for new alerts while it is open and does not use a third-party push service.
  • Voice: when you use Live AI voice features, audio or its transcript is processed to answer you. Speech recognition may run on your device or through your organisation's AI provider.

2. How we use information

  • To provide Qline's features: run your workspace, show your data to the people in your organisation, run the workflows you set up, and generate AI drafts and suggestions.
  • To keep Qline secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

3. Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional. Qline requests only the permissions needed for the features you enable:

What we store

  • For each new message Qline checks, we store the sender's name and email, the subject, the first 500 characters of the text, the received time and Qline's lead assessment, so the same message is not processed twice. Messages that are not leads are marked as ignored. We do not store a full copy of your mailbox or attachments.
  • IDs and links of the Sheets, Forms and calendar events Qline created, so it can update them and show them in your library.
  • Emails the AI assistant searches or reads at your request are not stored by Qline beyond the conversation in which you asked; the assistant's reply in that conversation is saved with your chat history.
  • Encrypted OAuth tokens.

Sharing of Google data

  • Google data is visible to members of the Qline organisation you connected it to.
  • If your organisation turned on AI lead triage, the sender, subject and up to 3,000 characters of a message's text are sent to the AI provider your organisation chose (for example OpenAI or Anthropic), using your organisation's own key, only to classify that message. When you ask the AI assistant to search or read your email, the matching messages (sender, subject, date and up to 4,000 characters of text) are sent to that same AI provider only to answer your request. Those providers process it under their own terms.
  • We do not transfer Google data to anyone else except as needed to provide or improve user-facing features, to comply with law, for security, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice.
  • We do not use Google data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine learning models.
  • Humans at Qline do not read your Google data unless you give permission for a specific message, it is needed for security or to comply with law, or it is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

Qline's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Microsoft and Slack

If you connect Microsoft, Qline uses Mail.Read, Mail.Send and Calendars.ReadWrite in the same ways described for Gmail and Google Calendar. If you connect Slack, Qline only posts alerts to the channels you choose and reads the channel list.

5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

  • Qline's servers and database run on Neon in the AWS Asia Pacific (Singapore) region.
  • Data is sent over HTTPS. OAuth tokens and AI keys are encrypted at rest. Each organisation's data is kept separate in the database.
  • No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of a breach as required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information while your account or organisation is active.
  • You can disconnect Google, Microsoft or Slack at any time in Settings → Integrations. This deletes the stored tokens, revokes Qline's access and stops syncing. Leads already found stay in your workspace until you or your organisation deletes them or asks us to. You can also remove access from your Google Account permissions.
  • You can export your organisation's data in Settings → Export.
  • To delete your account or organisation, email chrono07g@gmail.com from your account email. We delete the data within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law. Backups are removed on their normal rotation.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or object to how it is used, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and other laws that apply to you. Email chrono07g@gmail.com. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines or your local authority.

8. Children

Qline is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above. For important changes we will notify you in the app or by email.

AI business operating system

Your business, run by a team of AI agents that ask first.

Qline is a mobile app for small businesses. An AI CEO and specialist agents plan work, draft replies, sort new leads and organise tasks, contacts and documents. Anything that acts on your behalf waits for your approval.

What Qline does

One place for the work, the team and the approvals

Qline keeps your business in one workspace and lets AI agents help with it, using the AI provider and key your business chooses.

01

AI agents with limits

An AI CEO and specialist agents plan and draft. Every action is rated by risk, and risky actions need a person to approve them.

02

Lead inbox

Connect Gmail, Outlook or a Google Form. Qline finds messages that look like sales leads and ranks how urgent they are.

03

Work and contacts

Tasks, projects, contacts, notes and documents, shared with your team and roles.

04

Automations

Workflows that can create a Google Sheet or Form, add a calendar event, send an approved email or post to Slack.

05

Live AI

Talk to your AI CEO by voice and approve its suggestions without leaving the conversation.

06

Audit trail

Every important change is logged in a tamper-evident history you can export.

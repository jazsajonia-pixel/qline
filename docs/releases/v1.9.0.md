# Qline v1.9.0

**Content Studio: send video jobs to an outside editor through Google Drive, and let Qline check the result.**

- **Content Studio** is on Home under **Command Center**, next to Missions. "AI CEO" is now called "Command Center" everywhere you see it.
- **Drive folders.** Paste the links of one main folder and the folders inside it (source files, Ready for QA, optional Reports). Qline checks that each one really is inside the main folder, then indexes the files. It only reads your Drive; it never creates, moves, shares or deletes anything.
- **New handoff.** Pick source files, write a brief, choose a shape (9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5). Qline makes an editor PDF and a manifest for you to send to the editor.
- **Handoff Monitor.** Every job shows two things side by side: what Qline checked, and what the editor says. An editor saying "done" is never taken at face value; Qline opens the video in Ready for QA itself and checks its folder, type, size, name and checksum. Then you get a review task.
- **Reports.** Qline reads the editor's result files from the Reports folder, or you paste one into the job. Bad, old, replayed or mismatched reports are refused with a reason.
- **Kept up to date.** Qline looks for Drive changes every 10 minutes, flags jobs with no news for 3 days, and expires old ones. Notifications open the job directly.
- **Your receipt.** Save the editor PDF, the manifest and Qline's receipt from the job screen.

The editor's side is documented in [docs/23](../23-EDITOR_HANDOFF_SPEC.md).

**Server:** Content Studio needs API 1.9.0 with migrations 0020–0024. Until the server is updated, the app says "Content Studio is not on your server yet"; everything else works as in 1.8.0.

Not in this release:
- Qline putting the PDF and manifest into Drive for you;
- instant Drive push updates (needs a public HTTPS webhook address);
- publishing or approving videos.

Not yet checked on a physical device or with a real Google account and editor. See [docs/22](../22-CONTENT_HANDOFF_BASELINE.md) for what was and wasn't verified.

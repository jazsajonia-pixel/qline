# Qline v1.6.0 (draft — not released)

- **AI autonomy setting** (Settings → AI autonomy) with three levels: Supervised, Autopilot and Full autopilot.
  - Full autopilot asks for confirmation before it turns on.
  - Full autopilot has a daily limit on actions in connected apps.
  - Money, roles and data sharing are never automatic.
- **AI CEO delegation.** The CEO can hand work to other agents, and that work runs in the background.
- **New AI tools:** update companies and projects, create Slack channels, and set channel topics.
- **Auto agent.** Live picks the right agent for each message.
- **A voice that talks less.** It speaks a short summary while the full answer stays in the chat.
- **Follow-up suggestions** appear after every reply.
- **Voice animation** now follows the actual audio.
- **Send files to the AI.** Attach photos (up to 5 MB) or files such as CSV, Excel, PDF, Word or text (up to 10 MB), up to five per message. The AI can read them, save documents to your library, or import rows into contacts, tasks or leads after you approve.
- **Charts in chat.** Ask "show leads by source this month" for a chart computed from your own data. The AI can also chart numbers from a file you attached. Every chart says where its numbers came from.
- **New look.** A new citrine-on-spruce palette and an app icon that matches the Q mark inside the app (with a themed-icon layer for Android 13+).

- **Knowledge.** Library → Knowledge replaces Memory. Write down how the business works in categories; entries are private to you until you share them, keep a history of edits, and can be connected to contacts, companies, projects, tasks and other entries. The AI searches knowledge before answering and can suggest new entries and connections, which are marked "Added by AI".
- **Knowledge map.** See how entries, people, companies and projects connect, tap anything to inspect it, or centre the map on one item. A list view shows the same thing for screen readers.
- **Google Drive, Notion, GitHub and Stripe for the AI.** Search and read Drive files and Notion pages; create or update them with your approval. Read GitHub repos, issues and pull requests, and open issues or comment with your approval. See Stripe balance, payments and invoices, and create customers or draft invoices with your approval (Qline never charges, refunds or sends invoices).

Requires: migrations 0010, 0011 and 0012; the Slack `channels:manage` scope (reconnect Slack after adding it); and, for each new connector, the one-time owner setup in `docs/integration-setup.md` (enable Drive read scope; register Notion and GitHub apps; Stripe needs only a restricted key per workspace).

Not in this release: the knowledge context drawer and automatic knowledge injection with citations (K2), automatic knowledge capture (K4), a personal vault across businesses, and connector webhooks.

Behaviour change: deleting a knowledge entry is now a soft delete (hidden everywhere, history kept).

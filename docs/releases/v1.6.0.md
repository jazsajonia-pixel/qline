# Qline v1.6.0 (draft — not released)

- **AI autonomy setting** (Settings → AI autonomy) with three levels: Supervised, Autopilot and Full autopilot.
  - Full autopilot asks for confirmation before it turns on.
  - Full autopilot has a daily limit on actions in connected apps.
  - Money, roles and data sharing are never automatic.
- **AI CEO delegation.** The CEO can hand work to other agents, and that work runs in the background.
- **New AI tools:** update companies and projects, create Slack channels, and set channel topics.
- **Auto agent.** Live picks the right agent for each message.
- **A voice that talks less.** It speaks a short summary while the full answer stays in the chat.
- **Follow-up suggestions** appear after every reply.
- **Voice animation** now follows the actual audio.
- **Send files to the AI.** Attach photos (up to 5 MB) or files such as CSV, Excel, PDF, Word or text (up to 10 MB), up to five per message. The AI can read them, save documents to your library, or import rows into contacts, tasks or leads after you approve.
- **Charts in chat.** Ask "show leads by source this month" for a chart computed from your own data. The AI can also chart numbers from a file you attached. Every chart says where its numbers came from.
- **New look.** A new citrine-on-spruce palette and an app icon that matches the Q mark inside the app (with a themed-icon layer for Android 13+).

Requires: migrations 0010 and 0011 and the Slack `channels:manage` scope (reconnect Slack after adding it).

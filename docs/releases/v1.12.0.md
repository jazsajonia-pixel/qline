# Qline v1.12.0

**Live conversation with Qline's own voice: it starts talking sooner, and you can talk over it.**

- **Qline voice (on this phone).** Settings → Voice → Qline voice. A neural voice (Supertonic 3, or the faster Supertonic 2) that runs inside the app. Download it once (about 129 MB, Wi-Fi recommended); after that it is free and works offline. No other app, paid service or Gemini quota is needed. Delete it any time. English only: Filipino and Taglish words may sound wrong.
- **Faster replies.** In hands-free with the Qline voice, Qline starts speaking the first phrase while the rest of the answer is still being written.
- **Talk over it.** Just start talking to interrupt — no "stop" or button. The voice stops right away and Qline answers what you said next, knowing what you heard. Tapping the orb or Stop does the same.
- **Keeps going in the background.** A live conversation continues when you minimise Qline; the "Qline is listening" notification has End.
- **Benchmark this phone** in Settings → Voice measures how fast the voice runs on your phone, and the latest live conversation timings are listed there.
- The older hands-free mode is unchanged for the phone and cloud voices.

**Server:** API 1.12.0 adds real run cancellation (`POST /runs/:id/cancel`) and streams the spoken part of live replies. No database migration.

**Not verified yet:** no testing on a real phone. Sound quality, echo cancellation and how reliably interruption works on your phone are unknown until you try it. Details and limits: [docs/30](../30-REALTIME_VOICE.md).
